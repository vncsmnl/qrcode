# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People creating QR codes for personal sharing, small businesses, and professional use.

## Product Purpose

QR-FAST creates QR codes for links, plain text, Wi-Fi access, and WhatsApp conversations. Success means creating and exporting a usable code with little effort.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing app supports PNG and SVG export, image copy, compatible device sharing, QR color and background customization, and output size selection. Preserve these capabilities and the current Portuguese (Brazil) interface.

## Product Principles

- Make creating a QR code straightforward.
- Keep all four content types available.
- Keep customization and export options accessible.
- Preserve legibility and reliable scanning.
